# Muscle Pal positioning and artwork

The first three images should answer three questions in order. How do I perform this lift? Which muscles have I recently trained? How do I record it? Lead with the squat demonstration and its angle selector, then show the recovery dashboard and the logging sheet. The remaining images cover finding exercises, the body map, and model choice where suitable captures are available.

| Competitor | What its public listing emphasizes | Implication for Muscle Pal |
|---|---|---|
| Hevy | Workout logging, routines, charts, community, and logging from Apple Watch and Wear OS. | Demonstrate form and recovery before discussing routines and records. Describe watch support specifically as recovery and streak summaries. |
| Strong | Quick gym logging, flexible strength routines, statistics, and watch logging. | Show the real logging sheet and the quick Done action. Lead with the exercise-angle selector and recovery status. |
| MuscleWiki | Interactive muscle map, exercise demonstrations, a broad library, and workout planning. | Show how form guidance, a completed log, and per-muscle recovery connect. Avoid claims about having the biggest library. |

Sources: [Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/), [Strong](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strong-workout-tracker-gym-log/id464254577), [MuscleWiki](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musclewiki-workout-fitness/id1096827640).

The black artwork uses the app’s Poppins typography and lime accent. Short headlines sit outside the screenshots so the actual UI remains readable. The two-model banner supplies the Play feature graphic. The Apple Watch asset shows the native app with its recovery ring and streak.

The feature audit used the app routes, release documentation, bundled content, and Apple Watch target. The release includes form demonstrations, model choice, search and filters, favorites, recovery and readiness suggestions, routines, quick and detailed logs, history and progress, plate math, rest alerts, widgets, and data backup. The smartwatch target is watchOS. The release configuration reviewed has the initial features unlocked. The listing avoids pricing or advertising claims that could become stale.

This is a positioning recommendation based on public listings and the available captures. Conversion performance has not been measured.

## Screenshot redesign — 11 November 2026

Reviewed [SmartGym's current App Store presentation](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smartgym-gym-home-workouts/id922744883) and the supplied competitor comparison. SmartGym uses black backgrounds, short benefit statements, selective green emphasis, and prominent app/device imagery. The useful principles for Muscle Pal are one clear message per image, consistent typography, and actual product proof. Its awards and AI-personalization claims are not applicable to Muscle Pal.

The revised direction uses Muscle Pal's own lime, black/graphite surfaces, a numbered eight-page sequence, oversized white/lime headlines, and large native UI crops. Recovery leads with a full-body detail instead of a clipped map. Male and female demonstrations receive separate pages, and model preferences show both avatars. Phone and tablet sets now have identical feature coverage. No invented awards, ratings, user counts, or unsupported feature claims are included.
