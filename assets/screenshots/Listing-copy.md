## Full description for both stores

See how a lift is done, record your sets, and see how your training adds up. Muscle Pal brings 3D exercise demonstrations, a strength training log, and a muscle recovery map together in one app. No account required.

LEARN THE MOVEMENT  
Watch looping 3D demonstrations from multiple camera angles. Switch views to see the movement more clearly, see which muscles each exercise works, and read form cues and common mistakes. Find exercises by muscle group, equipment, or name. Choose a male or female demonstration model.

PLAN AROUND RECOVERY  
See an estimated recovery status for each muscle group based on your logged training. Explore the front and back body map and find exercises for muscles the app estimates are ready. Recovery estimates are training guides based on your log.

LOG THE WAY YOU TRAIN  
Mark an exercise done with one tap or record sets, reps, weight, and a note. Build reusable routines, save favorite exercises, time your rests, and calculate which plates to load. Choose kilograms or pounds.

SEE YOUR TRAINING HISTORY  
Review workouts on the calendar, edit past entries, and follow personal records, progress, streaks, and your weekly training goal.

TRAIN OFFLINE  
The bundled exercise library and workout log work offline. Export and import a backup to keep a copy of your training history.

**Append to the App Store description only**

CHECK RECOVERY ON APPLE WATCH  
See your recovery summary, streak, and estimated time remaining for resting muscles on your paired Apple Watch. Log your workouts on iPhone.
