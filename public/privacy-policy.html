Privacy policy

Muscle Pal for iOS and Android. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Your training stays on your device. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no purchases. We do not collect, store or sell personal data.

What the app stores on your device

Your workout logs, notes, routines, favourites and preferences (model, weight units, recovery speed, weekly goal) are saved in the app's local storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Exercise updates

The app ships with its exercise library built in and works offline. When it opens, it may check our content server for new or corrected exercises, at most every few hours, and download exercise text, videos and images. These requests contain no logs, notes, preferences or identifiers. Like any web request, the server receives your IP address; it is used only to deliver the files.

Backups

Export creates a JSON file with your logs, notes and preferences and opens your phone's share sheet; you decide where it goes. Import reads a backup file you choose and processes it on your device.

Deleting your data

Profile → Erase all logs removes your logs and notes from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Your phone's own backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data, according to your device settings.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for exercise updates and vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that processes personal data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
